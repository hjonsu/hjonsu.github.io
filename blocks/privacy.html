Blocks!

Privacy Policy · last updated 18 November 2026

Blocks! has no accounts, and your games stay on your iPhone. The only information it sends goes to Google, for ads.

On your iPhone

Your games, scores and settings are kept on your iPhone, and Blocks! never sends them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Ads play only when you choose to watch one, and they are limited to content suited to the app's age rating. Most come from Google AdMob.

To provide and measure its ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects information such as your IP address and approximate location, your device type and software version, a device identifier limited to the developer's own apps, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Blocks! never asks to track you across other companies' apps.

Google handles this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where privacy laws require it, you are asked for your choices about ads, and you can change them at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Children

Blocks! is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
