Blocks!

Privacy Policy · last updated 18 November 2026

Blocks! has no accounts and no analytics. Your games stay on your iPhone. The only information that leaves it goes to Google, for the ads you can choose to watch to revive.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved games, best scores, play statistics, the notes you find and your settings are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

When a game ends, you can revive by watching an ad. An ad plays only after you tap Watch ad, and ads are limited to content rated for general audiences or parental guidance.

The ads come from Google AdMob, except a video for Milk!, another app by the same developer. That video is stored inside Blocks!, so playing it sends nothing anywhere.

To choose, show and measure its ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects information such as your IP address (which gives your approximate location), your device's model and iOS version, an identifier that Apple provides to apps from the same developer, how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance data from the ad software. Blocks! never asks to track you across other companies' apps, so Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where privacy laws require it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, Google asks for or offers your choices about how your information is used for ads. There, you can change them at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Children

Blocks! is not directed at children under 13.
